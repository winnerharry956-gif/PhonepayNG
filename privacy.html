Privacy Policy PhonePay NG Last updated: 13 October 2026 1. Introduction PhonePay NG is a simple phone payment calculator designed to help users estimate the cost of buying a phone through instalment payments. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our website. 2. Information We Collect PhonePay NG does not require users to create an account. The calculator does not intentionally collect or store the phone prices or payment amounts you enter into the calculator. 3. How the Calculator Works Users can enter a phone price, an upfront payment, and a payment period. PhonePay NG then calculates an estimated remaining balance, monthly payment, and weekly payment. These results are estimates only. Actual financing costs and payment terms may differ. 4. Cookies and Advertising PhonePay NG does not currently intentionally use cookies to collect personal information. If advertising or analytics services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain any relevant changes. 5. Third-Party Services PhonePay NG may be hosted by third-party services, such as GitHub Pages. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and practices. 6. Children's Privacy PhonePay NG is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as PhonePay NG develops. Any updates will be published on this page. 8.

1. About PhonePay NG

PhonePay NG is a simple phone payment calculator designed to help users estimate the cost of buying a phone through instalment payments.

2. Information We Collect

PhonePay NG does not require users to create an account. The calculator does not intentionally collect or store the phone prices or payment amounts entered by users on our website.

3. How the Calculator Works

Users enter a phone price, an upfront payment, and a payment period to calculate estimated instalment payments.

5. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

6. Children's Privacy

PhonePay NG is intended for people who are able to understand and use a phone payment calculator responsibly. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

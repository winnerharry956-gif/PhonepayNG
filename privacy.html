Privacy Policy — PhonePay NG Last updated: 13 October 2026 PhonePay NG is a simple phone payment calculator designed to help users estimate the cost of buying a phone through instalment payments. Information We Collect PhonePay NG does not require users to create an account. The calculator does not intentionally collect or store the phone prices or payment amounts entered by users on our website. How the Calculator Works Users enter a phone price, an upfront payment, and a payment period. PhonePay NG calculates an estimated remaining balance, monthly payment, and weekly payment. These results are estimates only. Actual financing costs may differ. Cookies and Advertising PhonePay NG does not currently intentionally use cookies to collect personal information. If advertising or analytics services are added in the future, this policy may be updated. Third-Party Services PhonePay NG may be hosted by third-party services, such as GitHub Pages. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. Children's Privacy PhonePay NG is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Changes to This Policy This privacy policy may be updated as PhonePay NG develops. Contact For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the PhonePay NG website owner through the website where contact details are provided.

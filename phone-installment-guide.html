How to Calculate Phone Instalments in Nigeria: A Simple Guide

Buying a phone can be a big expense. If you are considering paying in instalments, understanding the numbers beforehand can help you plan your budget.

PhonePay NG is a free calculator that helps you estimate the remaining balance after an upfront payment and the amount you might budget for monthly or weekly payments.

1. Know the phone price

Start with the phone's advertised price. Confirm the price with the seller because prices may change. Check whether delivery, accessories or other charges apply.

2. Decide your upfront payment

An upfront payment is the amount you plan to pay at the beginning. Choose an amount that fits your budget while leaving money for food, transport, bills and other essential expenses.

3. Calculate the remaining balance

Formula:
Remaining balance = Phone price − Upfront payment

For example, imagine a phone costs ₦163,500 and you can pay ₦20,000 upfront.

₦163,500 − ₦20,000 = ₦143,500 remaining

4. Estimate the monthly payment

If you divide the remaining balance equally over six months:

₦143,500 ÷ 6 = ₦23,916.67 per month

This is a mathematical estimate. A real seller or financing provider may charge interest or fees, so the actual amount payable could be different.

5. Estimate the weekly payment

For a rough weekly budgeting figure, divide the monthly estimate by 4.345, the approximate average number of weeks in a month.

₦23,916.67 ÷ 4.345 ≈ ₦5,504.41 per week

This weekly figure is for budgeting only. It does not mean a seller will accept weekly payments.

6. Check whether you can afford it

Before committing, ask yourself:

  • Can I afford the payments after my essential expenses?
  • What happens if my income is delayed?
  • Are there interest charges or extra fees?
  • What happens if I miss a payment?
  • Have I checked that the seller and payment terms are legitimate?

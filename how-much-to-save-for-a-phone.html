How Much Should You Save Before Buying a Phone in Nigeria?

A new phone can be an important purchase, whether you need it for work, learning, communication or running a small business. Planning ahead can help you avoid spending money you need for essential expenses.

The right savings target depends on the phone you want, your income and your current expenses. There is no single amount that works for everyone.

1. Decide how much the phone costs

First, check the current price of the phone you want from a reputable seller. Prices can change, so confirm the final price before setting your savings goal.

Remember to include possible delivery costs, accessories or other necessary expenses.

2. Calculate how much more you need

Formula:
Amount still needed = Total target cost − Money already saved

For example, suppose your target phone costs ₦150,000 and you have already saved ₦30,000.

₦150,000 − ₦30,000 = ₦120,000 still needed

3. Set a realistic savings target

Choose a weekly or monthly amount that you can save after paying for essentials such as food, transport, housing and bills. Avoid setting a target that leaves you without money for emergencies.

4. Work out how long saving may take

If you need ₦120,000 more and can save ₦10,000 each month:

₦120,000 ÷ ₦10,000 = 12 months

This is a simple estimate, assuming you consistently save that amount and the phone price does not change.

5. What if you are considering instalments?

Some sellers or financing providers may offer payment plans. Before agreeing, confirm the upfront payment, total amount payable, interest, extra fees, payment dates and consequences of missing a payment.

An instalment plan is not automatically cheaper than paying upfront. Compare the total cost and make sure the payments fit your budget.

About PhonePay NG

Know your phone payment before you buy.

Who We Are

PhonePay NG is a free online calculator designed to help Nigerians estimate phone instalment payments.

What We Do

Enter a phone price, the amount you can pay upfront, and a repayment period of 3, 6, 9, or 12 months. The calculator estimates your remaining balance and monthly and weekly payments.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to help people plan their budgets before deciding whether a phone purchase is affordable.

Important Information

PhonePay NG is a calculator only. We do not sell phones, provide loans, arrange financing, or guarantee approval for instalment purchases.

All results are estimates for budgeting purposes. Actual payments may differ because of interest, fees, and the terms offered by a financing provider. Confirm all final terms directly with the provider.

Free to Use

You can use our calculator without creating an account. You do not need to provide your BVN or make a payment to calculate an estimate.
